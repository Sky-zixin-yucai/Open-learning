# 行为准则 (Code of Conduct)

## 🌍 项目愿景 / Project Vision

### 中文
本项目是一个**渐进式学习项目**，记录每一种从零开始的学习旅程。我们以从未学习过的领域为目标，从零探索未知的世界。

我们以**渐进式学习**为宗旨，完整记录学习过程中的错误与成长。来到这里的旅者将感受到世界的多样性，并愉快地开始每一天的学习。

只要你有电脑就可以开始。如果你想参与，我们非常欢迎——只需为你创建的项目负责。这里是项目的天堂，每一个项目都属于那位努力的创造者。

### English
This is a **Progressive Learning Project**, documenting every learning journey from scratch. We take entirely unfamiliar subjects as goals, embarking on explorations of unknown worlds from ground zero.

With **progressive learning** as our core principle, we faithfully record the complete journey of mistakes and growth. Travelers who arrive here will experience the diversity of the world and joyfully begin each day of learning.

All you need is a computer to get started. If you wish to participate, you're most welcome—simply take responsibility for the project you create. This is a paradise of projects, where each one belongs to the hardworking creator behind it.

## 📜 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

我们致力于为所有参与者提供一个友好、安全的环境，无论其年龄、体型、残疾、民族、性别认同与表达、经验水平、国籍、个人外貌、种族、宗教或性取向如何。

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all participants, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## ✅ 预期行为 / Expected Behavior

- **尊重差异**：尊重不同的观点、经验和学习方式
- **鼓励学习**：支持从错误中学习，将挑战视为成长机会
- **分享精神**：乐于分享自己的学习过程和经验
- **耐心相助**：对初学者保持耐心，提供建设性帮助
- **诚信记录**：真实记录学习过程，不掩饰错误或困难

- **Respect Differences**: Respect differing perspectives, experiences, and learning styles
- **Encourage Learning**: Support learning from mistakes, view challenges as growth opportunities
- **Share Spirit**: Be willing to share your own learning process and experiences
- **Patient Assistance**: Maintain patience with beginners, provide constructive help
- **Honest Documentation**: Truthfully record the learning process, do not hide mistakes or difficulties

## 🚫 不可接受的行为 / Unacceptable Behavior

- 任何形式的骚扰、歧视或贬低性言论
- 恶意批评他人的学习进度或方法
- 故意提供错误信息或误导性指导
- 窃取或不当使用他人的学习成果
- 任何使他人感到不适、威胁或恐吓的行为

- Harassment, discrimination, or derogatory remarks of any kind
- Malicious criticism of others' learning progress or methods
- Intentionally providing incorrect information or misleading guidance
- Plagiarizing or improperly using others' learning achievements
- Any behavior that makes others feel uncomfortable, threatened, or intimidated

## 🌱 关于学习项目的特别说明 / Special Notes for Learning Projects

本项目专门记录**真实的学习过程**，这意味着：
1. 你将看到不完美的代码和错误的尝试
2. 学习路径可能不是最优或最直接的
3. 不同人的学习速度和方式可能不同
4. 重点在于**过程**而不仅仅是**结果**

我们鼓励：
- 分享你的学习困惑和突破
- 用建设性方式指出可以改进的地方
- 为他人提供鼓励和支持
- 尊重每个人的学习节奏

This project specifically documents **real learning processes**, which means:
1. You will see imperfect code and failed attempts
2. Learning paths may not be optimal or most direct
3. Different people may learn at different speeds and in different ways
4. The focus is on the **process** not just the **outcome**

We encourage:
- Sharing your learning confusions and breakthroughs
- Pointing out areas for improvement in constructive ways
- Providing encouragement and support to others
- Respecting everyone's learning pace
