# 开源项目行为准则
Open Source Project Code of Conduct

---

## 1. 价值观声明 / Statement of Values

### 中文
本社区致力于培养高创造力的个体，为其提供锐利的工具与优质的协作生态。我们相信，个体的创造性贡献最终将回馈于更广泛的技术与社会共同体。

我们的系统构建遵循三条核心心法：
1.  **命名，而非形容**：追求精准、具象的表达，减少模糊性。
2.  **呈现，而非解释**：优先展示可验证的事实、代码与数据，让成果自己说话。
3.  **指认，而非概括**：关注可操作的具体细节和微观改进，避免空泛讨论。

在这里，我们树立的旗帜是：**“创造高于消费，心法高于功能，自由高于归属。”**

### English
This community is dedicated to nurturing highly creative individuals by providing them with sharp tools and a high-quality collaborative ecosystem. We believe that individual creative contributions will ultimately benefit the broader technology and social commons.

Our system of construction follows three core principles:
1.  **Name, Do Not Describe**: Strive for precise, concrete expression, reducing ambiguity.
2.  **Present, Do Not Explain**: Prioritize showing verifiable facts, code, and data—let the work speak for itself.
3.  **Identify, Do Not Generalize**: Focus on actionable specifics and microscopic improvements, avoiding vague discussions.

The banner we raise here is: **"Creation over consumption, methodology over features, freedom over affiliation."**

## 2. 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

我们作为成员、贡献者和维护者承诺，让参与本项目的每个人都能免受骚扰，无论其年龄、体型、残疾、民族、性别认同与表达、经验水平、国籍、个人外貌、种族、宗教或性取向如何。

我们承诺以有助于建立开放、友好、多元、包容和健康社区的方式行事和互动。

As members, contributors, and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## 3. 预期行为 / Expected Behavior

以下行为有助于我们实现社区的价值观和愿景：
The following behaviors are expected and contribute to realizing our community’s values and vision:

*   **以具体行动践行核心心法 / Enact Core Principles Through Concrete Actions**
    *   在提交代码、撰写文档或提出建议时，力求精准命名（如变量、函数、标题）。
    *   When submitting code, writing documentation, or making proposals, strive for precise naming (e.g., variables, functions, titles).
    *   通过可运行的代码、可复现的案例或清晰的日志来“呈现”问题或方案。
    *   “Present” problems or solutions through runnable code, reproducible cases, or clear logs.
    *   在评审或讨论时，“指认”具体的代码行、文档段落或逻辑步骤进行反馈。
    *   During reviews or discussions, “identify” specific lines of code, documentation paragraphs, or logical steps for feedback.

*   **尊重创造过程与个体差异 / Respect the Creative Process and Individual Differences**
    *   理解学习、探索和创造是非线性的，尊重不同的技术路径和思维模式。
    *   Understand that learning, exploration, and creation are non-linear; respect different technical paths and thinking modes.
    *   对处于不同阶段的贡献者（尤其是初学者）保持耐心，提供具有建设性、可操作的建议。
    *   Be patient with contributors at different stages (especially beginners), offering constructive and actionable advice.
    *   乐于分享过程中的失败经验、中间状态和决策逻辑，而不仅仅是最终成果。
    *   Be willing to share failures, intermediate states, and decision logic from the process, not just final outcomes.

*   **建设性地沟通与协作 / Communicate and Collaborate Constructively**
    *   即使存在分歧，也保持尊重和礼貌，聚焦于技术事实和项目目标。
    *   Remain respectful and courteous even in disagreement, focusing on technical facts and project goals.
    *   乐于接受针对工作的善意批评，并将其视为改进的机会。
    *   Be open to well-intentioned criticism of your work and view it as an opportunity for improvement.
    *   在有能力时，主动帮助他人澄清问题、理解代码或定位错误。
    *   Proactively help others clarify questions, understand code, or locate errors when you are able.

## 4. 不可接受的行为 / Unacceptable Behavior

以下行为被认为不可接受，且与我们的价值观相悖：
The following behaviors are considered unacceptable and contrary to our values:

*   **违反核心心法的行为 / Actions Violating Core Principles**
    *   使用模糊、带有主观评判性的语言形容问题而非具体指认（如：“这段代码很烂” vs “第X行的循环可能导致性能瓶颈”）。
    *   Using vague, subjectively judgmental language to describe problems rather than identifying them concretely (e.g., “This code is terrible” vs. “The loop on line X may cause a performance bottleneck”).
    *   只提出批评或需求，但不提供任何可验证的代码、数据、日志或可复现的步骤。
    *   Raising criticism or demands without providing any verifiable code, data, logs, or reproducible steps.
    *   进行空泛的、不指向任何具体工作成果的概括性赞扬或贬低。
    *   Offering generic praise or criticism that does not point to any specific work product.

*   **损害协作环境的行为 / Behaviors Harming the Collaborative Environment**
    *   **骚扰性的言论或玩笑**：包括与个人特征相关的侮辱、贬损评论，公开或私下的性暗示，蓄意恐吓，跟踪，未经同意的拍照或录音，以及持续干扰对话。
    *   **Harassing speech or jokes**: Including insults or derogatory comments related to personal characteristics, sexual innuendo in public or private spaces, deliberate intimidation, stalking, unauthorized photography or recording, and sustained disruption of discussions.
    *   **歧视性行为**：基于本准则第2条所列任何个人特征的区别对待或排斥。
    *   **Discriminatory behavior**: Differential treatment or exclusion based on any personal characteristic listed in Section 2 of this Code.
    *   **恶意行为**：故意传播错误信息，恶意破坏代码或文档，窃取他人成果并声称自己所有。
    *   **Malicious conduct**: Deliberately spreading misinformation, maliciously sabotaging code or documentation, plagiarizing others’ work and claiming ownership.
    *   **人身攻击**：针对个人而非其工作内容进行攻击。对他人学习速度、方法或当前能力水平的恶意嘲讽。
    *   **Personal attacks**: Attacking a person rather than their work. Malicious ridicule of others’ learning pace, methods, or current skill level.
    *   **不当关注**：在对方明确表示拒绝后，仍持续进行不必要的私下联系（私信、邮件等）。
    *   **Unwanted attention**: Continued unwanted private contact (DMs, emails, etc.) after a clear request to stop.

## 5. 维护者的责任与权力 / Maintainer Responsibilities and Authority

项目维护者（或拥有合并权限的贡献者）有责任维护本行为准则的标准。他们有权也有责任在社区的任何空间（如仓库、讨论区、邮件列表、线下活动）中，对不符合本准则的行为进行纠正。

具体权力包括：
Project maintainers (or contributors with merge permissions) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct. They have the right and responsibility to address any behavior in any community space (e.g., repository, discussion forum, mailing list, in-person event) that they deem inconsistent with this Code.

Specific authorities include:

1.  **内容管理 / Content Moderation**：临时或永久删除不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、Wiki编辑、议题或拉取请求。
2.  **参与权管理 / Participation Management**：暂时或永久地限制任何其认为行为不当、具有威胁性、攻击性或有害的参与者在本项目中的互动权限（包括但不限于评论、提交、创建议题/拉取请求）。
3.  **解释权 / Interpretation Authority**：在必要时，对准则的具体条款进行解释，以应对复杂情况。所有解释都应基于准则精神，并以促进一个更安全、更包容的社区为目标。
    - **Temporary or permanent removal** of comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, or pull requests that are not aligned with this Code.
    - **Temporary or permanent restriction** of interaction privileges (including commenting, committing, creating issues/PRs) for any participant whose behavior is deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
    - **Authority to interpret** the specific terms of this Code as necessary to address complex situations. All interpretations should be based on the spirit of the Code and aim to foster a safer, more inclusive community.

## 6. 举报与执行流程 / Reporting and Enforcement Process

### 如何举报 / How to Report
如果您遭受或目睹了不可接受的行为，或者对某些行为感到不安，请立即通过以下渠道联系项目维护团队。我们鼓励尽早报告，以便我们能够及时提供支持。
If you are subjected to or witness unacceptable behavior, or feel uncomfortable about certain conduct, please contact the project maintainer team immediately through the following channels. We encourage early reporting so we can provide timely support.

**主要举报渠道 / Primary Reporting Channel**
- 发送邮件至行为准则专用邮箱：**`skyzixinyucai@126.com`**
- Email the Code of Conduct dedicated mailbox: **`skyzixinyucai@126.com`**

**备用联系途径 / Alternate Contact**（如主邮箱无法访问时）
- 通过 GitHub Issues 联系维护者团队，标题请注明“Code of Conduct - Private”
- Contact the maintainer team via GitHub Issues with the subject “Code of Conduct - Private”

在举报时，请尽可能提供：
When reporting, please provide if possible:
*   涉及的人员（请勿公开转发私人沟通记录）。
*   The individuals involved (please do not publicly forward private communications).
*   行为发生的地点（如GitHub议题链接、聊天频道、会议名称）。
*   The location where the behavior occurred (e.g., GitHub issue link, chat channel, meeting name).
*   行为的具体描述和相关证据（如截图、日志，注意隐私）。
*   A description of the behavior and any relevant evidence (e.g., screenshots, logs, mindful of privacy).
*   事件发生的大致时间。
*   The approximate time of the incident.

**我们承诺对举报人的身份和举报内容严格保密。**
**We commit to keeping the reporter’s identity and the report content strictly confidential.**

### 处理流程 / Handling Process
1.  **确认接收 / Acknowledgment**：维护团队将在 **48小时** 内确认收到您的报告。
    The maintainer team will **acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours**.
2.  **调查与审议 / Investigation & Deliberation**：维护团队将进行必要、谨慎、公正的调查，并与相关方进行私下沟通。调查周期视情况而定，但我们将尽力在 **1周内** 提供进展更新。
    The maintainer team will conduct a necessary, discreet, and fair investigation, communicating privately with relevant parties. The investigation timeline varies, but we will strive to provide a progress update **within 1 week**.
3.  **决定与执行 / Decision & Action**：基于调查结果，维护团队将决定采取的措施（例如：警告、要求公开道歉、临时禁言、永久封禁）。处理决定将私下通知直接相关方。
    Based on the findings, the maintainer team will decide on actions to take (e.g., warning, request for public apology, temporary mute, permanent ban). Decisions will be communicated privately to directly involved parties.
4.  **结果告知 / Outcome Notification**：举报人将被告知处理决定已作出，并获悉其核心结论（在不违反他人隐私的前提下）。
    The reporter will be notified that a decision has been reached and informed of its general outcome (without violating others’ privacy).

如果您对处理流程或结果有异议，可以向维护团队请求复审。
If you disagree with the handling process or outcome, you may request a review from the maintainer team.

## 7. 适用范围 / Scope

本准则适用于所有代表项目或项目社区的场景，包括但不限于：
This Code applies within all project spaces and in all situations where an individual is officially representing the project or its community, including but not limited to:

*   项目托管平台上的空间（如 GitHub 仓库内的 Issues、Pull Requests、Discussions、Wiki、代码注释）。
*   Spaces on the project hosting platform (e.g., GitHub repository’s Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, Wiki, code comments).
*   项目官方使用的沟通渠道（如邮件列表、Slack/Discord/论坛、社交媒体账号）。
*   Officially used communication channels (e.g., mailing lists, Slack/Discord/forums, social media accounts).
*   项目组织或认可的线上、线下活动。
*   Project-organized or endorsed online or offline events.
*   在其他场合使用项目的官方身份（如使用项目邮箱发表言论）。
*   Using an official project identity in other contexts (e.g., speaking using a project email address).

个人私人空间或个人社交账号上的行为，一般不在本准则管辖范围内，除非其行为对项目社区成员造成了明确、直接的影响。
Behavior in personal private spaces or on personal social media accounts is generally outside the scope of this Code, unless such behavior has a clear and direct impact on project community members.

## 8. 关于“渐进式学习”与“不完美”的特别说明 / Special Note on “Progressive Learning” and “Imperfection”

本项目尤其欢迎记录真实、渐进的学习与创造过程。这意味着：
This project particularly welcomes the documentation of real, progressive learning and creation processes. This implies:

*   您将看到不完美的、实验性的代码和失败的尝试。
*   You will see imperfect, experimental code and failed attempts.
*   贡献者的学习路径和解决方案可能不是最优的。
*   Contributors’ learning paths and solutions may not be optimal.
*   不同贡献者的进展速度和技能背景差异很大。
*   The pace of progress and skill backgrounds of contributors will vary greatly.
*   我们高度重视过程中的思考、调试和迭代记录，其价值不亚于最终成果。
*   We highly value the documentation of thinking, debugging, and iteration during the process, which is as valuable as the final outcome.

因此，在适用本准则时，维护团队将充分考虑这一背景，旨在**保护健康的探索过程**，而非要求所有人都呈现“完美”的结果。
Therefore, when applying this Code, the maintainer team will fully consider this context, aiming to **protect a healthy exploration process** rather than requiring everyone to present “perfect” outcomes.
