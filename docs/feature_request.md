## 功能目标 / Feature Goal
*清晰描述这个功能希望达到什么目的，解决当前项目的什么限制或用户什么需求。*
*Clearly describe the purpose of this feature and what limitation or user need it addresses.*

## 解决方案/架构设想 / Proposed Solution or Architecture
*描述你设想的实现方案，可以包含技术要点、模块设计或伪代码。*
*Describe your proposed solution. You may include technical points, module design, or pseudocode.*

## 替代方案考虑 / Alternatives Considered
*你是否考虑过其他实现方式？请简要说明并解释为何未采纳。*
*Have you considered alternative approaches? Briefly explain and why they were not chosen.*

## 附加信息 / Additional Context
*添加任何其他有助于理解此功能请求的截图、链接或说明。*
*Add any other context, screenshots, or links about the feature request here.*

## 贡献意愿 / Contribution Intent
*请表明您对此建议的参与意愿，这有助于社区协作。*
*Please indicate your willingness to participate, which helps community collaboration.*
*   [ ] 我计划并愿意实现此功能。 | I plan and am willing to implement this feature.
*   [ ] 我希望参与讨论，并可能提供部分帮助。 | I wish to participate in the discussion and may provide partial help.
*   [ ] 我仅提出建议，希望由其他人实现。 | I am only making a suggestion and hope others can implement it.
